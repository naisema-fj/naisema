## Scope

The public site shell (issue #18): header and mobile menu, footer, homepage, the six area pages and the Article reading page. Visitor mode: Read. Audience and constraints: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every public page is a letter from home to Fijians abroad: signed, dated and honestly marked with what was reviewed. It refuses the category default of a photo hero over a grid of image cards.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white sheets on a slightly deeper sand ground. Deep teal ink for headings, links and the airmail edge; masi brown as the edge's second colour; near-black body text. The airmail edge is a thin diagonal band in teal and masi brown at the head of the page and of each letter. Postmark lines: small, letter-spaced, ruled date and Review Label lines in teal, flat and factual, never seal-like. Tapa depth stays restrained: one masi geometric strip in the footer. System sans for interface; a book serif stack for long reading.

STORY: The visitor reads a welcome letter, sees what each area holds and what isn't open yet, opens an Article, and sees who wrote it, when, and exactly what it was reviewed for.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The airmail band across the top; the wordmark Na iSema at left with the six areas inline (a menu on phones). A wide letter sheet: "Bula vinaka" as the salutation, the welcome statement at reading size, signed "Na iSema". Below it, the six areas addressed like envelope fronts in a 3 by 2 grid, each with a postmark-style availability line. The primary action, "Read the latest", sits in the letter's sign-off.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Without JavaScript, envelope fronts lift and their airmail edge darkens on hover and keyboard focus; the mobile menu is a native disclosure.

FORM: Letters home; position 5 on the ordered list (masi bands, sevusevu sequence, ibe mats, the Fijian hymnal, letters home, reef charts, radio listings); seed key 64c7cb06 (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
