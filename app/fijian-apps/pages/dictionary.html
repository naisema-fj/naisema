    How to use this dictionary

    Explore a word through its meaning, a Fijian phrase, related words, and a question to think about. Some entries are still being checked.

    1. Search a Fijian word or English meaning, or browse Themes.
    2. Open Tuvavosa to see how a word works in a complete phrase.
    3. Follow related words or a further-reading link to explore a distinction.
    4. Read the English meaning, then see what the Fijian example adds.
    5. Use the question on an entry to look more closely at its meaning or use.
    6. Browse Vosavosavakaviti for Fijian proverbs and idioms.

    About Tuvavosa

    A yavu carries the main meaning of a phrase; duru add to or limit that meaning. This guide looks at matanauni and matavu as two phrase types. Fijian phrase roles do not always match English word classes. Check the full phrase before deciding a word’s role.

    In “na vale levu qo”, “vale” is the base and “levu” adds description. Check the full phrase before deciding its role.
