# Lessons are Content Items of their own, not Learning Layers

Learn will hold interactive lessons that aren't built on a video, starting with those previewed on apps.naisema.com (body words and possession, the drua and the vale vakaviti). We make a Lesson a new Content Item type in Learn rather than stretching Learning Layers to work without a video. A Learning Layer is defined by its clip: its Segments are timed spans, its Tokens and Annotations anchor to them (ADR-0011) and its Immersion Route starts from watching. A lesson has none of these, and making the video optional would leave most of that model empty and every rule about it conditional. As a Content Item, a Lesson gets what every Content Item already has: one URL at `/learn/{slug}`, immutable Revisions with review fingerprints (ADR-0006), Review Approvals scoped by Review Type (ADR-0003; language always, cultural when flagged, so Knowledge Holder Approval where it is needed), Rights Records for its images and sources, and the single eligibility decision at request time (ADR-0007). It reuses the Activity model and the Completion Rule from Learning Layers, adding Activity kinds as lessons need them (labelling a picture, building a word or sentence from blocks), and a Learner's progress is keyed to the Lesson Revision, as it is to the Learning Layer Revision, so that learner data survives later revisions in the same way. A lesson's Fijian points to Expressions, never to unreviewed dictionary drafts. Lessons are the one public page type that needs interaction, so a Lesson page may hydrate a small island for its Activities under the same strict content security policy (no inline scripts or styles); everything else on it is server-rendered and works without JavaScript, with each Activity's accessible text alternative. We rejected a separate "lesson app" outside the CMS, because its content would bypass review and rights, which is what NAISEMA's public claims rest on.

Lessons are outside Phase 1a as scoped in ADR-0002, so building them needs the written change note the decision log requires.
