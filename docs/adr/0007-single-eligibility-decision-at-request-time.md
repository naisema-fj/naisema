# One eligibility decision, evaluated at request time

Whether anything public may be served (a page, search result, saved item, caption or video playback token) is decided by one server-side function over the Revision: published, asset ready, exact-revision approvals present, rights current and not withdrawn. Because it runs on every request, rights expiry takes effect without a background unpublish job; a daily scheduled job only sends expiry warnings. To meet the 15-minute removal target (AC-03, VAC-06), public HTML is edge-cached for at most 5 minutes and purged on withdrawal, the search index is rebuilt from eligible Revisions on publish and withdraw, and every Stream video requires signed playback with tokens of about 10 minutes, so an already-open page loses playback shortly after withdrawal.
