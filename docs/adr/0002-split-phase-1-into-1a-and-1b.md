# Split Phase 1 into 1a and 1b

The PRD (v5) places the full video immersion stack, review-gated CMS, learner accounts and every directory (provider catalogue, creator profiles, radio timetables, marketplace directory, E-zine issues) in one Phase 1. For a small team we split it: **1a** delivers the CMS with rights and exact-revision review gates, video immersion (VID/VCMS/VTECH, VAC-01–10), Learner Accounts, public browsing of the six areas and minimal directories; **1b** adds radio timetables, the marketplace directory, the fuller provider catalogue and E-zine issue assembly. Video immersion carries the most architectural risk and is the PRD's declared core capability; the deferred items are mostly further CMS content types that need no new architecture. This is a recorded scope amendment under the PRD's delivery contract.
